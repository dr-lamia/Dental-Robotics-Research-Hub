# Literature search strategy

Sources to search and document: PubMed/MEDLINE, Scopus, Web of Science, IEEE Xplore, ACM Digital Library, Embase, Google Scholar and Crossref. Also inspect cited references, citing articles, preprints and linked supplementary materials.

Core concepts: (robot* OR autonomous OR robotic-assisted OR haptic OR teleoperat* OR digital twin) AND (dent* OR odont* OR oral OR maxillofacial OR endodont* OR orthodont* OR prosthodont* OR implant*).

Record query and run date for each database; deduplicate by DOI/title; independently screen title/abstract and full text; flag primary studies versus reviews; check GitHub, Zenodo, Figshare, Dryad and article data availability statements for code/datasets.

This is a living research index, not a completed systematic review.
