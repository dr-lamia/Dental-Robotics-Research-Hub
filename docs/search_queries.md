# Reproducible discovery queries

**Scope:** dental and maxillofacial robotics, including clinical systems, preclinical evaluation, planning, control, simulation, and relevant open data/code.

## PubMed (adapt syntax before reuse elsewhere)
```
(("Robotics"[Mesh] OR robot*[tiab] OR telerobot*[tiab] OR "robot-assisted"[tiab] OR "robot guided"[tiab] OR "autonomous surgery"[tiab])
AND
("Dentistry"[Mesh] OR "Dental Implants"[Mesh] OR "Oral Surgical Procedures"[Mesh] OR dent*[tiab] OR tooth[tiab] OR teeth[tiab] OR implant*[tiab] OR endodont*[tiab] OR orthodont*[tiab] OR prosthodont*[tiab] OR maxillofacial[tiab] OR "oral surgery"[tiab]))
```

## IEEE Xplore / ACM / Scopus / Web of Science
Search (robot* OR robotic-assisted OR teleoperat* OR autonomous) AND (dental OR dentistry OR tooth OR implant OR endodontic OR orthodontic OR maxillofacial OR crown preparation). Include conference papers and technical reports.

## Repositories and datasets
Search GitHub, Zenodo, Figshare, 4TU.ResearchData, Mendeley Data, OSF, Hugging Face and institutional repositories. Use exact paper titles and author names to find linked code or supplementary data.

## Screening protocol
Record database, search string, date, hits, DOI, decision, reason, and reviewer. Perform citation chasing and document duplicates. Do not claim PRISMA completion until full searches, dual screening, and flow counts are documented.
