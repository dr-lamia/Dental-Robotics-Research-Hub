# Dental robotics research opportunities

## Robotic crown-preparation benchmark
Combine annotated tooth/arch 3D meshes, planned preparations, bur geometry and robotic trajectories. Report surface deviation (RMS/RMSE), preparation depth and taper, margin accuracy, toolpath error and collision events. Distinguish simulation from experiments and clinical tests.

## AI-driven planning
Evaluate an AI-generated preparation plan against human-designed CAD reference. Use independent dental expert assessment and standardized geometry metrics.

## Physics-based dental digital twin
Model material and instrument interactions, force/torque sensing, collision detection and uncertainty. NVIDIA Isaac Sim, ROS 2 and MoveIt are generic enabling tools; specialized tooth cutting physics must be developed and validated.

## Data gap
No complete publicly verified paired dataset of clinical crown-preparation scans, robot control trajectories and cutting forces was established in the initial collection. Do not assume absence; perform targeted repository and supplementary-material searches.
