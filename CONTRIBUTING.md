# Contributing research resources

Open an issue or pull request. For each paper supply full citation, DOI, year, specialty, study design and a verifiable link. For code and datasets supply canonical URL, version, license, data restrictions, and connection to the article.

Do not upload copyrighted article PDFs, private patient data, restricted CBCT/DICOM or other assets without permission.

Candidate discoveries are reviewed before being added to the verified catalog.
